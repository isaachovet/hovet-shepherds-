We are the shepherds

Trusted care for ministry leaders.

We are Isaac and Donia Hovet, Standing Stone Shepherds. We walk with pastors, ministry leaders, and their spouses through confidential, ongoing, Christ-centered care—at no cost to them.

Our ministry is donor supported. Some people come here because they need a safe place to be honest. Others come because they want to help make that care possible.

Meet your shepherds

We understand ministry from the inside.

We bring more than two decades of pastoral experience, graduate formation, prayer, and hard-won discernment to the quiet work of walking with leaders. Our experience includes lead pastoral ministry, church revitalization and transition, discipleship development, staff leadership, preaching and teaching, and mentoring leaders across generations.

We know both the joys of ministry and the strain that can be difficult to explain to anyone outside it.

Isaac is especially attentive to spiritual discernment, conflict, institutional pressure, and the power of honest language. Donia brings perceptive wisdom around relationships, leadership, family systems, and the emotional currents beneath the surface.

Together, we hope to offer a grounded, deeply Christian presence: truthful, gentle, and shaped by the peace of Christ.
